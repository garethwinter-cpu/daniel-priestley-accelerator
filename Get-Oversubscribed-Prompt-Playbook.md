# The Get Oversubscribed Prompt Playbook

All 29 AI prompts from *The Science of Growing Customers* with Daniel Priestley, lesson by lesson.
Replace anything in <angle brackets> with your own details. Daniel's tip: end any prompt with
"ask me questions so that you can advise" — let the AI interview you before it answers.

## Part 1 — Oversubscribed Principles

### Lesson 1 — Only Oversubscribed Businesses Make Profit

```
Based on my business model and my capacity, how many clients can I take on each year and deliver a full and remarkable experience?
```

### Lesson 2 — First Make Your Market, Then Make Your Sales

```
Based on what you know about me and my market, who is the ideal customer persona I would be best to target and at what price point? Consider that I want to target an ICP with available budget to spend (either discretionary income or an existing budget). I want them to easily justify their spending through savings, upside or experience. I want them to be a pleasure to work with and I can easily deliver the outcomes they want.
```

```
I want to run a marketing campaign to attract and engage my ideal customer persona. I want them to signal their interest in solving a problem or getting an outcome. They will either register for an event, complete an assessment, request a sample, join a discussion group, or some other free step. Suggest 10 specific campaigns that I could run to get people to signal their intention of working with me for free.
```

### Lesson 3 — It Takes 11 Interactions, 7 Hours, 4 Locations for People to Be Pre-Sold

```
Search online for quality content that features me. How many pieces of quality content can you easily find? How many hours can someone spend freely watching or reading my content to get to know me?
```

```
Based on what you know about my core offer, what are 10 beliefs that someone would have to have in order for them to enthusiastically buy from me?
```

### Lesson 4 — Decommoditise Through Philosophy & Experience

```
I want to share the deeper philosophy that drives what I do professionally. I want to share a statement that makes people realise there's a bigger game that I'm playing — beyond the products and services that I sell. Based on what you know about me, what do you think this bigger game would be? What would this statement be?
```

```
I want to create an experience that leaves a remarkable impression for my clients and potential clients. They will feel compelled to share it on social media along with photos and videos. This experience will differentiate me from my competitors, putting me in a category of one in the eyes of my prospects. Give me ten ideas to explore.
```

### Lesson 5 — Buying Environments Causes Buying

```
Help me build a calculator that shows return on investment for buying my product or service, assuming the buyer is my ideal customer persona. Help me build a logical case I can calculate on a spreadsheet.
```

```
I suspect my potential clients have a dream moment they imagine happening in the future that I'm able to get them closer to. It's like a movie they play in their mind. When I tell them I can help them achieve this movie, they get excited to buy from me. What's the fantasy they have in their head?
```

### Lesson 6 — People Buy to Solve Problems

```
Considering what I do professionally, what is the pain that I help people to solve? What is the obstacle that I remove? What is the prize that I deliver? Why do people see my offer as a path of least resistance? And what is the pain that people experience if they don't buy?
```

### Lesson 7 — People Buy What They Want, Not What They Need

```
Considering what I do professionally, how would I explain it to someone whose highest value is freedom? Security? Adventure? Family? Fitness? Health? Wealth? Status?
```

### Lesson 8 — People Buy What Others Want to Buy, Not What Others Want to Sell

```
Who are examples of high-profile successful people who buy and recommend services like what I offer?
```

```
If I could showcase any brand ambassador who would instantly make my offer more desirable, who would be my perfect brand ambassador?
```

### Lesson 9 — People Have No Imagination

```
What would be the most compelling way to show what my business does visually? Then create a full brief for me to give to a designer to improve my brochures, landing pages and social media accounts to attract more high-quality ideal customers.
```

### Lesson 10 — People Are Not Proactive

```
Create a sales script for me to follow up warm leads that have expressed interest in working with me. Create a list of common objections people might have to working with me, and how I would handle them.
```

```
Write 10 examples of powerful calls to action that I can put at the bottom of my social media posts to get people to DM me, click a link or signal their interest in working with me.
```

```
Create a nudge email for me to follow up with people on my database who have gone cold. I want to elegantly re-engage their interest without seeming desperate.
```

## Part 2 — Oversubscribed Actions

### Lesson 11 — Campaign Team

```
I'm launching a new business. Our marketing plan will follow Daniel Priestley's Oversubscribed methods — perfect repeatable week, quarterly spotlight and big message. I will need a team to help me execute. The key roles will be: Key Person of Influence (the reputation and face), Head of Growth (generating leads and sales), Head of Delight (making sure customers are cared for), and Head 'Swiss Army Knife' (getting general things done). Help me to recruit, onboard and align these team members.
```

### Lesson 12 — Product & Service Ecosystem

```
I'm productising my business. My core intellectual capital is about how to <XYZ> and my ideal customer is <ICP>. Help me to develop a product and service ecosystem using Daniel Priestley's Ascending Transaction Model. Gift: something of value I can easily give away for free. P4P: a low-risk first step to learn about and experience what I do. Core Offer: a full and remarkable solution all packaged up. P4C: an ongoing service, subscription, membership or upsell.
```

### Lesson 13 — Perfect Repeatable Week

```
I am developing short-form videos to promote my business. Based on what you know about me, help me develop scripts that will engage my perfect clients. I want to hook attention, deliver something of value and then get people to comment a word in exchange for longer, more detailed content. Ask me any clarifying questions and then begin creating short-form video scripts.
```

```
I am developing long-form videos to promote my business. Based on what you know about me, help me develop scripts that will educate my perfect clients on my value proposition. I want to hold their attention, deliver real value and get them to then click a link to complete a self-assessment on my website. Ask me any clarifying questions and then begin creating long-form video scripts.
```

```
I am developing an online quiz / assessment that will highlight why my ideal customer would get value from working with me. Help me to develop this self-assessment tool so that when the right person takes it they feel confident in signing up to work with me.
```

```
Build me a dashboard to measure my weekly: Leads, Appointments, Presentations, Sales. The dashboard should allow me to track and measure my activity over the next 3 months.
```

### Lesson 14 — Quarterly Spotlight

```
I want to run a special quarterly marketing campaign designed to promote my business, reengage past prospects and attract attention from new buyers. I want it to be special and unique from my other marketing. Ask me questions and make suggestions for this campaign. Then create a detailed campaign marketing and logistics plan for me to execute.
```

```
I'm hosting a special event for my business in 6 months. I want a special guest speaker who will be a drawcard for my clients. This person should be successful but not a superstar. They probably have 10,000 to 100,000 social media followers, a book they've written and a powerful story/ideas that my clients will want to hear. Ideally they live close to <city> and they will agree to speak for less than <budget>. Suggest 10 options.
```

### Lesson 15 — Annual Big Message

```
I want to be a guest on more podcasts and YouTube channels. I'm looking for small and niche platforms to start with — ideally 500–5,000 subscribers. Suggest 20 podcasts or YouTube channels I could go on that would be ideal for my brand.
```

```
I'm appearing as a guest on a podcast. I want to launch in with a powerful opening hook that captures the audience and makes them want to watch or listen to the whole episode. Craft an opening hook based on what you know about me.
```

### Lesson 16 — Celebrate & Innovate

```
I have collected data from potential customers who have engaged an online self-assessment called <name>. Analyse the data provided and then: share the customer segments represented; highlight the ICP I should be targeting; improve my marketing message to attract more of my ICPs; suggest ways to improve my offer and the correct pricing for this ICP; create a basic financial forecast and timeline if I follow up correctly with my current leads; share any other insights that might be valuable.
```

```
Create a detailed description or infographic about my ICP so I can show my sales team and they will completely understand our ideal customer and what makes them engage and buy from us.
```
